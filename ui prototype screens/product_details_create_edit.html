Technical Description & Material Profile

High-tensile woven polyester strapping engineered for rigid cargo lashing. High shock absorption properties with minimal elongation under heavy transit vibration. Replaces conventional steel strapping without splintering risks and eliminates oxidation transfer to lumber and galvanized surfaces. Cross-woven yarns resist transverse notch shearing.
